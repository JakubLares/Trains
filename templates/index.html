💡 How to Use

  1. Go to cd.cz and search for your train
  2. Click on your train and then "All information about the train"
  3. Copy the URL from your browser and paste it above
  4. Enter your destination station name
  5. Configure when you want to be notified (e.g., 60 minutes before arrival)
  6. Click "Test Connection" to verify it works
  7. Click "Start Monitoring" to begin
  8. You'll receive a desktop notification at the configured time before arrival
